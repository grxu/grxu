Needed before the render

  1. SignUp brand colour. I have no official hex for it. The frames show its ribbon as a dashed grey line for now.
  2. Logo files, SVG preferred. Truvio, SignUp, Axtension, SKsoft and DynamicWeb. I place them into these frames at exact size. They stay out of every AI render.
  3. A reversed Truvio logo for Deep Violet. The guidelines keep the wordmark in Deep Violet, and a Deep Violet logo disappears on a Deep Violet field. Is there an approved white version? The fallback is the standard logo on a white card.
  4. Screen spec and file deadline from the AV vendor. Frame rate, codec and the date they need the file. The Summit opens 11 October, 13 days from today.
  5. Two quick confirms. The brief spells SkSoft, and my notes say SKsoft. The frames use SKsoft. The button uses Yellow Gold with Deep Violet text.

The idea

Colour

Each legacy brand keeps its own colour for Panel 1. The four colours braid into one line, and the line opens into the Truvio gradient bands.

Background

White before the merge. Deep Violet floods out from the braid at 7.2 s and holds to the end. A white wipe at 17.4 s hands back to the start of the loop.

Layout

Panel 1 sits left-aligned: four names on four separate rows. From Panel 3 on, everything is centred. Four things become one.

Timeline

Seedance 2.0 caps a clip at 15 seconds, so the render splits into two shots at the 8 second mark.
