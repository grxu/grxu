# Higgsfield prompt: three steps

Truvio PIM LinkedIn single-image ad, 1:1 (1080x1080) only. A clean numbered layout: the hook on top, three steps in three columns, a cropped mapping screen under step 3, the reassurance line, then the lockup and CTA. The LinkedIn headline and body sit outside the image. Grady sets the hook, numbers, step text, footer, CTA and lockup by hand. He places the official AppSource badge at step 1 and the mapping screen, rebuilt flat from the real screen. Higgsfield makes the full plate: the Deep Violet ground with the step 2 and step 3 icon tiles in place, and empty violet everywhere else.

## How the image matches the copy
- "in 3 steps": three equal columns, each with a visual, a number and two short lines. The eye reads them left to right, like a short checklist.
- "Install from AppSource": the official Microsoft badge. Buyers know it from AppSource, so it proves the app is listed there.
- "Run the guided setup wizard": a settings cog with a green tick reads as setup done.
- "Choose what syncs, and which way": two straight arrows pointing opposite ways, in two colours. The direction is the point.
- "Try the field mapping yourself" (ads headline): the mapping screen crop sits under step 3, so the viewer sees the screen the demo lets them try.
- "No code. No middleware.": the image shows no code, servers or connectors. The footer line carries it.

## Decisions (12 Oct 2026)
- Higgsfield makes the full plate. The badge and the mapping screen are placed by hand, so the plate holds the ground and the two tiles only. The plate gives the tiles real light and shadows on violet.
- Step 1: Microsoft's official AppSource badge, placed by hand. Not generated and not imitated. Nano Banana distorts logos, and a look-alike of a Microsoft mark has no place in a paid ad.
- Mapping screen: the real screen, rebuilt flat from Grady's screenshot, like the BC card in the messy item card. Kept white at full strength. A 34 percent violet tint read lilac and muddy at feed size.
- Size: 1:1 only, same as the messy item card.
- Three columns instead of a vertical list. The badge is wide (216x72 in the mock). In a list it pushes the step text right and leaves no room for the mapping crop. Each column is 290px, which fits the badge next to two 104px tiles.
- Cog with a tick instead of a wand with sparkles. Sparkles now read as AI and would suggest AI runs the setup.
- Two straight arrows instead of circular sync arrows. A circle reads as refresh. Straight arrows show direction, the "which way".
- Glyphs in Deep Violet with Electric Green for the tick and the return arrow. Tiles in off-white #F8F9FE.
- Numbers: 48px circles in the secondary gradient at 25.4 degrees, numerals in Deep Violet. It echoes the gradient underline in the messy item card.

## Clean plate option
Use it after two batches with wrong symbols. Run Prompt A with the two symbol sentences replaced by: "Both tiles are blank, with no symbol." Then place the vector cog and arrows from the mock on the tiles in Photoshop. Set them to Multiply and add a 1px Inner Shadow at 30 percent for the letterpress dent. Shapes and colours are then exact. The mock can be exported to .svg and .ai on request.

## Brand check before you comp
- Ground: sample it, target #400685. The cleanest comp cuts the tiles out with their shadows and lays them on a flat #400685 fill, each shadow on its own Multiply layer. To keep the generated ground, correct it with a masked Curves layer, which keeps the shadows.
- Tiles #F8F9FE. Glyph inks #400685 and #2DB34A. Correct a drifting green with a masked Hue/Saturation layer.
- Cog: eight even teeth, round hollow, tick inside. Arrows: straight, opposite ways, equal length and weight.
- No letters, numbers or gibberish anywhere. No logos.
- AppSource badge: the official file, unchanged. Follow Microsoft's usage rules on clear space and minimum size. The stand-in is 216x72, so scale the real badge to 72px tall and check it reads on violet.
- Swap Arvo Bold for Museo Slab in the hook and the numerals.
- The lockup in the mock is rendered from the official Truvio_logo_white.svg. Place the official asset anyway.
- Mapping screen: rebuilt from the real screen, checked with the product team before launch.
- Copy zones clear. 65px safe margin on all sides.

## Fixes if a batch misses
- Gradient or vignette on the ground: add "perfectly flat, even violet ground from edge to edge" at the start.
- Tiles come back flat, like the reference graphic: add "real physical card tiles, photographed, with visible thickness and a soft contact shadow" at the start. Still flat: drop the reference image and rely on the percentages.
- A third tile fills the empty step 1 slot: add "exactly two tiles, nothing to their left" at the start.
- Cog turns into a flower or sun, or the teeth are uneven: change it to "a simple settings cog icon with eight short square teeth". Still wrong: use the clean plate option.
- Arrows curve into a circle: add "two straight parallel arrows, not curved, not a circle".
- Sparkles or a wand appear: add "no sparkles, no stars, no wand" at the start.
- Letters or numbers appear on the tiles: move "There are no readable words, letters or numbers anywhere in the image" to the start.
- Hex codes appear as text: delete the hex codes, keep the colour names and correct the colour in Photoshop.
- Grey or black shadows: add "the shadow is tinted deep violet, not grey".
- Tiles soft at this size: run the same prompt with "each tile is 30 percent of the frame width, the two tiles centred side by side" in place of the layout sentences, then scale them down in the comp.
- Looks like a cheap stock mockup: add "high-end editorial still life, real paper, soft directional daylight".
