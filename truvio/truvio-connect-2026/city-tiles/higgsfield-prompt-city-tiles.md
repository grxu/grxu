# Higgsfield prompt: Truvio Connect 2026, City Tiles

Option B for the Truvio Connect 2026 LinkedIn campaign. It replaces Miniature Cities (decided 26 Oct 2026). Each city is an isometric miniature tile of a real neighbourhood, in real colours. Higgsfield makes the tile on a flat light grey ground with a soft shadow, and the top of the frame stays empty. Grady swaps in the brand background in Photoshop and sets the city, date and CTA by hand. Sizes 1:1 (1080x1080) and 4:5 (1080x1350). Four cities: London (10 Nov), Copenhagen (12 Nov), Eindhoven (24 Nov), Stockholm (26 Nov).

## Decisions (26 Oct 2026)
- Real-world colours and materials. No brand colours in the generation. Brand comes from the background, type and tags added by hand.
- One hero landmark per city plus 3 or 4 real neighbours, compressed. No invented geography.
- No sun, clouds, weather, date or city name in the image. Those made the reference read as the Nano Banana weather-tile trend.
- No venue on the tile.
- Tiny attendees with lanyards walk toward the hero. Lanyards are plain dark grey, so Grady can recolour them to Deep Violet.
- The references Grady shared are mood only. They are not attached, since the model copies their text and their London buildings.

## Settings
- Model: Nano Banana Pro. It handles dense architecture and real landmarks best of the image models on the account.
- 1:1 and 4:5, run separately. Fallback for 4:5 is 3:4, trimmed 90 px.
- Resolution 2K. Batch of 1 per prompt. Grady reviews and calls rerolls.
- References: none for London. After London passes, the London winner goes in as a style reference for the other three. If a city copies London buildings, rerun it with no reference.

## Shared paragraphs

Every city prompt is the city paragraph, then the four shared paragraphs below, then the layout lines for the size.

**Style**
Premium 3D architectural miniature, like a high-end museum scale model with a clean, slightly simplified finish. Real-world colours and materials throughout: true stone, brick, copper, glass and water colours, natural green trees. Matte surfaces with soft real reflections on glass and water only. Crisp detail from edge to edge, no depth of field, no tilt-shift blur.

**Camera, tile and light**
Classic isometric three-quarter view from above, the camera about 35 degrees above the ground and turned 45 degrees, so the square tile sits as a diamond with one corner pointing at the camera. The tile is a thick slab with clean cut edges, and the water and ground are cut cleanly at the edge to show a thin cross-section. The tile sits on a plain, flat, light neutral grey background, evenly lit from edge to edge, with no gradient, no texture and no horizon. The tile casts one soft shadow down and to the right onto the background. Soft late-morning sunlight from the upper left, gentle shadows, clear even exposure.

**Attendees**
Eight tiny attendees walk in small groups toward the hero landmark, mid-step. Each figure is no taller than a ground-floor doorway, wears simple business-casual clothes in white, grey and navy, and wears a plain dark grey lanyard with a small plain white badge. Simplified miniature faces, no real person.

**Exclusions**
No text anywhere: no city name, no date, no numbers, no signs, no shop names, no labels, no logos, no flags. No sun, no clouds, no sky, no weather icons. No people other than the attendees and a few pedestrians, no hands, no props outside the tile, nothing else in the frame.

## London

### City paragraph
A square isometric miniature tile of the real neighbourhood around Tower Bridge in London. The Thames runs diagonally across the tile in calm grey-blue water. The hero is Tower Bridge at the front centre, spanning the river, built correctly: exactly two Victorian Gothic towers in pale grey stone with steep pointed roofs and small corner turrets, two high walkways joining the towers near the top, the road deck between them, and pale blue suspension chains sweeping down to both banks. On the north bank behind the bridge stands the Tower of London: the square pale stone White Tower with four corner turrets inside low curtain walls and green lawns. Behind it rise the City of London towers: the Gherkin, a rounded glass bullet-shaped tower with a diagonal diamond grid, and the Walkie-Talkie, a top-heavy glass tower that widens toward its top. On the south bank stands The Shard, a slim glass pyramid that tapers to a jagged point, the tallest object on the tile. Riverside paths lined with trees, a few streets of low stone and brick buildings, two red double-decker buses, three black cabs and one small river boat.

### 1:1 layout
The tile spans 86 percent of the frame width, centred. Its front corner sits at 94 percent of the frame height. The tip of The Shard reaches no higher than 32 percent from the top. The top 30 percent of the frame is empty grey background, kept clear for a headline.

### 4:5 layout
The tile spans 92 percent of the frame width, centred. Its front corner sits at 95 percent of the frame height. The tip of The Shard reaches no higher than 36 percent from the top. The top 34 percent of the frame is empty grey background, kept clear for a headline.

## Copenhagen

### City paragraph
A square isometric miniature tile of the real harbour neighbourhood around Nyhavn in Copenhagen. The hero is the Nyhavn canal at the front centre: a straight canal of calm water lined on one side by a row of narrow 17th-century townhouses with gabled and stepped roofs, painted in real Nyhavn colours (ochre, rust red, pale blue, mustard yellow and off-white), each with white-framed windows. Old wooden sailing ships with bare masts are moored along the cobbled quay. The canal opens onto the wide harbour. On the harbour front at the mouth of the canal stands the Royal Danish Playhouse, a modern building of dark glass and dark timber on a wooden boardwalk. Behind the canal houses stand Amalienborg, four identical pale rococo palaces around an octagonal cobbled square with an equestrian statue at its centre, and behind them the Marble Church with its large round green copper dome, the tallest object on the tile. Across the harbour stands the Copenhagen Opera House, a low pale building with a huge flat roof that cantilevers out over the water. Copper-green spires, a few cyclists, two harbour boats.

What worked
- All five landmarks are right and sit where they really are. Tower Bridge has two towers, two walkways and blue chains. The White Tower, Gherkin, Walkie-Talkie and Shard all read at a glance.
- Real colours and materials, with no brand colours.
- Restrained: two buses, a few cabs, one boat. No sun, no clouds, no city name.
- The background is flat warm grey (#D3CECA) from top to bottom, with one clean shadow to the right.

What missed
- Headline zone. The Gherkin reaches 17% from the top and the Shard 22%. The target was 30% clear. The tile spans 92% of the width, not 86%.
- Tile base. The slab shows brown soil in cross-section, which reads like a geology cutaway. The references use a clean pale plinth.
- Attendees. They stand in two groups at the bridge's north end instead of walking, the lanyards don't show, and one figure has a malformed head. At 1080 px each figure is about 10 px tall, so they won't read in the feed.
- A small Union flag on the White Tower, despite "no flags".

Changes to try in v2 (not yet run)
- Layout: "The tile spans 74 percent of the frame width, centred. Its front corner sits at 92 percent of the frame height. The tallest building reaches no higher than 32 percent from the top. The top 30 percent of the frame is empty grey background, kept clear for a headline."
- Base: "The tile is a thick, clean, pale stone-grey plinth with smooth sides, like a museum model base. No soil, no cutaway layers." Grady can tint the plinth to a brand colour later.
- Flags: add "no flagpoles" to the exclusions.
- Attendees: drop them, or keep them knowing they only show at full size.

## Avoid list
Nano Banana Pro has no negative prompt field. The exclusions sit inside the prompt.

## Fixes if a render misses
- Tower Bridge gets extra towers or turns into London Bridge: "exactly two towers, the Victorian bascule bridge with two high walkways next to the Tower of London"
- Text, signs or a city name appear: move the exclusions paragraph to the very start of the prompt
- Sun, clouds or sky appear: "plain flat light grey background only, no sky elements"
- Too cluttered: "fewer buildings, wide open streets and lawns between the landmarks"
- Figures too big: "the attendees are tiny, each no taller than a ground-floor doorway"
- The tile tilts to a front view: move the camera paragraph to the very start
- Another city copies London: rerun without the style reference
