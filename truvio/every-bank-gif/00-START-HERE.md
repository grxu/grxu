# Every bank GIF: Higgsfield kit

A 10-second looping GIF for the F&O Compliance LinkedIn ads. One bank at a time slides onto a violet stage: a tower, then a rotunda, then a classical bank. One payment file sits centre front the whole time. It gets folded by hand to fit each bank's slot: a pleat for the tower, a tube for the rotunda. At the classical bank it jams. Premium studio 3D, handmade stop-motion look, locked camera. 1080x1080, 12 fps.

## How to run it
1. Open `RUN-ORDER-bank-formats-gif.md`.
2. **Step 1: T0.** Nano Banana Pro, 1:1, 2K, batch 4, with the layout ref attached. Everything else is built from T0, so get it right before moving on.
3. **Steps 2 to 7:** the other six stills. Each one attaches T0, or the still named in the step.
4. **Step 8: V1.** Seedance 2.0, first and last frame. This is the pilot clip. If the paper melts instead of folding, stop and adjust before spending credits on the rest.
5. **Steps 9 to 16:** the remaining clips, the Photoshop prep, then the slides.

## Settings at a glance
- Stills: Nano Banana Pro · 1:1 · 2K
- Video: Seedance 2.0 (Kling as backup) · 1:1 · highest resolution · shortest length
- Every clip: locked camera, stop-motion on twos, no motion blur, no hands
- Negative prompt: in Step 8 of the run order. Use it for every clip.

## Rules that matter
- Keep the copy and the Truvio logo out of every generation. They're added as static layers in After Effects at the end.
- R2 has no tape. A tape band that isn't in R0 fades in during the video.
- Slides are made one bank per clip, and entrances are reversed exits. A tower and a rotunda in one clip morph into each other.
- Keep grain and noise out. They wreck the GIF's 5 MB limit.
