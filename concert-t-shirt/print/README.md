## Notes

- **Front.** The black flames and the two black slots inside the red block are cut-outs, so the black shirt shows through. That keeps the front to three inks.
- **Back.** This is the big black group from the artwork (three circles with flame cut-outs, above the III), printed in white. In the poster, parts of the III are hidden behind the B, the G and the red block. Those parts are rebuilt by mirroring the III's fully visible bottom half, so the top serifs and slot tops match the bottom.
