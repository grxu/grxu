# Prompts for the two flower layouts (Higgsfield app, Nano Banana Pro)

**Settings:** model **Nano Banana Pro**. Attach **New option 2** (the
four-corner watercolour with the yellow rose) as the reference image. Aspect
**1:1**, highest resolution offered (4K, or 2K if that's the max). Generate a
few, then download the best one of each as PNG.

## Layout 1: U-shaped cradle → upload to `layout-1-cradle/`

```
Using the attached watercolour flower illustration as the style reference, paint a new arrangement using only three of its flowers: the deep red peony, the blue bearded iris and the yellow rose. Remove the pink peony and the cream peony completely. Keep the same loose, hand-painted watercolour style, colours and brushwork.

Composition: an asymmetric U-shaped cradle that opens upward. Flowers sit on the left side, along the bottom and on the right side only, with nothing above. Leave the upper-middle area completely empty (about half the image width and a third of its height) so a bold letter can be added there later. Make it clearly asymmetric, not mirrored: the red peony is the largest bloom, low on the left; the yellow rose is medium-sized, on the right and a little higher; the blue iris is smaller, rising up the left side beside the empty area. Green leaves and one or two small buds trail along the bottom to link them.

Plain solid white background, no shadow, no paper texture, no frame, no text or letters.
```

## Layout 2: asymmetric diagonal → upload to `layout-2-diagonal/`

```
Using the attached watercolour flower illustration as the style reference, paint a new arrangement using only three of its flowers: the deep red peony, the blue bearded iris and the yellow rose. Remove the pink peony and the cream peony completely. Keep the same loose, hand-painted watercolour style, colours and brushwork.

Composition: an asymmetric diagonal sweep. A heavy cluster at the bottom-left (the large red peony with the yellow rose tucked beside it) trails up diagonally towards the top-right, thinning out into leaves and a bud, with the smaller blue iris at the top-right. Leave a clear horizontal band across the centre of the image completely empty (about half the image width and a third of its height) so a bold letter can be added there later. Nothing should be mirrored or centred.

Plain solid white background, no shadow, no paper texture, no frame, no text or letters.
```
