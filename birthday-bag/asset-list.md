# Asset list – rendered separately, assembled on the template

Each asset is rendered on its own on a plain, flat, light background so the background is easy to remove in Higgsfield. Then it gets placed on the 2050 × 1656 canvas following the chosen layout (A1 or A2).

The order matters: lock the style and the princess first, then use her as the style reference for everything else so every asset looks like it came from the same book.

## 1. Main character – the princess

| # | Asset | A1 | A2 | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Character sheet (front, ¾, side, back) | ✓ | ✓ | Locks her face, hair, dress and proportions. Use it as the reference for every pose below. |
| P1 | Standing, leaning in, waving | ✓ | | Biggest asset; about 45% of the bag's height. |
| P2 | Sitting on a log, swinging her legs | | ✓ | |

Original character, *Rapunzel-like*, based on our daughter: a 3-year-old toddler princess with her real hair (straight, black, just below the shoulder blades) plus a thin flowered side braid and a flower crown, a lilac dress, barefoot. Not the Disney design.

## Assembly rules

- Background (E1) fills the whole canvas, past the red line.
- Faces and the four key animals stay inside the blue line.
- The name and "3" get typeset last, as real type in the final file, not generated.
