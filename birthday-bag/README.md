# Birthday souvenir bag – "[Name] is turning 3"

A souvenir bag for our daughter's 3rd birthday. It shows an original, Rapunzel-like toddler princess in the woods with animals. A giraffe, an elephant, a capybara and a horse are always in the scene; other animals are optional. The same design goes on both sides of the bag.

## Print specs (from the vendor template)

| | Pixels @ 200 ppi | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Full canvas (includes the 1 cm fold/seam) | 2050 × 1656 | 26 × 21 cm |
| Red dashed line, the finished bag face (cut) | ~1890 × 1496 | ~24 × 19 cm |
| Blue dashed line, the safe area | ~1732 × 1339 | ~22 × 17 cm |

- The background must run to the canvas edge. Anything between the red line and the edge is folded into the seam.
- The name, the "3", the princess's face and the four key animals must sit inside the blue line.
- Aspect ratio is about 5:4 (1.238). Generate at 5:4 or larger and crop to the canvas.
- `template/cut-and-safe-guides.png` is the vendor's guide layer. Lay it over drafts to check placement.
- `template/vendor-sample.png` is the vendor's example layout.

## Layout options

Flat wireframes on the real canvas, with the vendor's cut (red) and safe (blue) lines over them. `layouts/layouts-A-B-C.png` shows all three side by side.

- **A · Text centre** (`layouts/layout-A-text-centre.png`): name and "3" in a forest clearing; the princess, the four key animals and the woodland friends wrap around like a wreath.
- **B · Princess left, text right** (`layouts/layout-B-princess-left.png`): the vendor sample's structure, mirrored; the princess is big, the animals parade under the text.
- **C · Title top, parade below** (`layouts/layout-C-title-top.png`): storybook-cover title; the princess centre stage, flanked by the animals.

**Chosen direction: A, made more dynamic** (`layouts/layouts-A1-A2.png`):

- **A1 · Ring dance** (`layouts/layout-A1-ring-dance.png`): a loop from big to small around the clearing. The princess is big in the foreground and leaning in, the horse trots behind her, and her braid sweeps along the bottom with the capybara sitting on it. The elephant sprays water toward the "3", and the giraffe's neck curls up the right side toward the name.
- **A2 · Peekaboo forest** (`layouts/layout-A2-peekaboo-forest.png`): depth from cropping and peeking. The giraffe's neck reaches in from the trees on the left, the elephant is big and cut off by the bottom-right edge, the princess sits on a log, the horse lies down, and the capybara sits in a pond.

The name is set in type in the final file, not generated by Higgsfield. It stays `[Name]` until we have the spelling.
