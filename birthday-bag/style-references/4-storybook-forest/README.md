# Style 4 – Storybook forest (layered, grainy, glowing)

Look, from a reference Grady shared in chat (a forest-path illustration, signed and marked "all rights reserved", so it isn't stored here):

- Flat, layered digital shapes, each with a soft gradient.
- Grainy risograph texture and fine halftone dots over everything.
- Glowing light coming through the treetops from behind. Distant layers are pale and hazy; near layers are rich and dark.
- Palette: luminous lime and yellow-green light, then emerald, teal and mint, with deep blue and indigo leaves in the foreground. Warm accents in golden yellow and rosy pink.
- Chibi characters: big round heads, simple eyes with a white highlight, round rosy cheeks, a thin darker outline and a light rim along the edges.

The prompts describe this look in words only. Once a render is approved, it becomes the style reference for every other asset.
