**Style block** (already included in every prompt below):
> modern flat vector illustration for preschoolers, bold simple geometric shapes, flat saturated colour with no gradients and no texture, tiny minimal faces (dot eyes, small curved smile, rosy cheek dots), thin black hand-drawn doodle accents (sparkles, plus signs, small dots, motion ticks), playful and friendly, palette of tangerine orange, bubblegum pink, sky blue, sunny yellow, grass green and violet on a white background

**Watch for:** in this style the animals get very simplified. Check that the giraffe, elephant, capybara and horse are still easy to recognise at a glance (long neck and spots, trunk and big ears, a round brown body with a blunt snout, a mane and tail).
