# Style 3 – Drawing (pencil child, single-colour hatched animals)

**Attach as style references:**
- **1–3 photos of our daughter** (face reference, for P0 and every princess render; uploaded to Higgsfield only, never committed)
- `style-references/3-drawing/e850d7383dd15f8faf6d8a5200849eaf.jpg` (yellow lion with child: the clearest example of the technique and the doodle accents)
- `style-references/3-drawing/24a0b678d46cdabcaf88e6f19519420b.jpg` (blue wolf: fur hatching, pencil child)
- `style-references/3-drawing/b4eb668b8c5b34cdd841a770a2007f18.jpg` (four colours: shows how each animal gets its own hue)

**Style block** (already included in every prompt below):
> hand-drawn children's book illustration, the child drawn in soft black-and-white graphite pencil with rosy pink cheeks, each animal drawn in a single bold colour with dense fine directional pen hatching (fur and manes as thousands of fine strokes), clean white paper background with lots of empty space, a few loose single-line doodles of clouds, grass and little flowers

**Colour plan:** every character keeps one colour, so the bag reads as a set.

| Character | Colour |
|---|---|
| Princess | graphite pencil; her braid drawn in her natural hair colour with fine hatching, tiny pink flowers in it as the accent |
| Giraffe | sunflower yellow |
| Elephant | cobalt blue |
| Capybara | terracotta orange |
| Horse | rose pink |
| Small woodland animals and birds | mint green or lilac |

**Size:** in this style, the animals are drawn big next to the child (gentle giants). That suits layout A1 and A2: the princess is smaller than the giraffe, elephant and horse, and they protect her.

**Watch for:** the hatching is very fine. If the vendor says their print can't hold fine lines, add *slightly bolder strokes* to the prompts. Single assets stay on pure white so they cut out cleanly; the forest plate (E1) fills the whole bag, because the bag is full-bleed.

## Round 1 – style test

### P0 · Princess character sheet (16:9)
```
Character turnaround sheet of one toddler princess in a hand-drawn children's book illustration style, based on the little girl in the attached face photo: keep her face shape and features. Front view, three-quarter view, side view and back view, standing in a row, evenly spaced, full body, same size, on clean white paper.
She is about 3 years old with toddler proportions: big round head, chubby cheeks, small happy closed eyes or tiny dot eyes, sweet smile, rosy pink cheeks. She is drawn entirely in soft black-and-white graphite pencil: a short puffed-sleeve dress, bare feet, a small flower crown. Her extremely long hair is one thick braid in her natural hair colour, drawn with dense fine directional pen hatching, trailing past her feet and curling on the ground, with a few tiny pink flowers in it as the only accent colour.
Loose, confident linework, lots of empty white space. Consistent character in every view. No text, no labels, no signature.
```

### S1 · Giraffe test (3:4)
```
One big, gentle giraffe in a hand-drawn children's book illustration style, full body, standing, long neck curving gently upward and leaning forward, head tilted down with a kind, sleepy smile. Drawn entirely in a single sunflower-yellow colour with dense fine directional pen hatching: the fur, the mane and the rounded spots are all built from thousands of fine strokes, the spots slightly darker in the same yellow. Clean white paper background, lots of empty space, a few loose single-line yellow doodles of grass at its feet. No text, no signature.
```

### E1 · Forest plate (5:4)
```
A light, airy forest scene in a hand-drawn children's book illustration style, wide, seen straight on, filling the entire frame edge to edge on warm cream paper. Tall trees at the left and right edges and leafy branches across the top corners drawn with fine directional pen hatching in soft sage green, a soft hatched grassy ground across the bottom, loose single-line doodles of clouds, grass tufts, little flowers and leaves in soft green, yellow and pink. The centre is an open, lightly textured cream clearing: space for characters and a title. No characters, no animals, no people, no text, no signature.
```
