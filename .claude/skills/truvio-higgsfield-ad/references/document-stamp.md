# Document Stamp formula

A document (invoice, payment slip, envelope, passport) with a rubber-stamp verdict on a Deep Violet ground. Used across the F&O Compliance campaign: REJECTED, FINED, FILED, CLEARED.

## Colours
- Ground: Deep Violet #400685, flat.
- Problem stamps (REJECTED, FINED): Prometheus Red #F76336. Briefs often carry peach #F4A989, which is off palette. Flag it every time and offer Prometheus Red.
- Good-news stamps (FILED, ACCEPTED, CLEARED): Electric Green #2DB34A. Red on a success word reads as an error.
- Paper White #FFFFFF. Placeholder bars Silver #CFD2D4, bold bars and small tags Dark Gray #6D757A.
- Headline in the house off-white #E6E8FC.
- Confirm the stamp colour each time anyway. Grady decides.

## The stamp
- Heavy slab-serif capitals inside a bold rectangular double border. It echoes Museo Slab (Arvo Bold in mocks).
- Angle about 25 degrees, a nod to the brand gradient angle of 25.4 degrees. Set it exactly in post.
- Rising from lower left to upper right means the right end sits higher. Don't promise it lands on something at the bottom right. Mock it first.
- Real ink: slightly uneven pressure, a few small gaps and speckles, the paper lines faintly visible through the ink (multiply). It stays inside the paper edges.
- At feed size it must be the second thing read after the headline. Faint, speckled ink reads weak. Push "bold, saturated, freshly inked".
- Clean plate option: generate without the stamp, then set it in Illustrator (Arvo Bold or Museo Slab, exact hex, 25.4 degrees) with a grunge mask in Photoshop at Multiply 90 to 95 percent.

## The document
- Modern, minimal invoice: INVOICE in bold black sans capitals top left, a blank light grey logo square top right, two address blocks, a line-item table with thin rules, a totals block bottom right with one bolder bar, and two footer lines.
- Every other line is a silver placeholder bar. The only readable words are the title and the stamp word.
- Flat-lay from directly above, tilted about 5 degrees, soft contact shadow toward the bottom right (light from the top left).
- A PDF variant adds a folded top-right corner and a small Dark Gray "PDF" tag. It makes "PDF invoices" visible, at the cost of a third word.

## Hero variations that worked as concepts
- The pile: four or five fanned invoices, all stamped. It shows "keep" or repetition.
- Bounced back: an open envelope with the invoice half out, the stamp on the front and a round return-arrow sticker (icon only, no words).
- Before and after: a tilted PDF stamped REJECTED, then a gradient arrow, then a straight app window stamped ACCEPTED in green. It's the only one that shows the fix. It's busier, so it fits better as a second ad in a set.
- Others explored in the campaign: a paper plane nose-dived and stamped, passport-style country stamps, banknotes and fees, a scanner belt (CLEARED).

## Animation
- Stamps always go in After Effects. AI video fades them in and the letters wobble.
- The pile: four timed drops, the hero last and only once, falling toward the surface. Or start with the bottom two sheets already down.
- Bounced back: the envelope slides out, stops as if it hit a wall, and slides back. The stamp and sticker land in AE as it settles.
